A Greek Island Novel of Second Chances and Family Secrets

Some secrets keep a family together. Others only teach it to wait.

Eleni Vasiliou has spent her whole life in the taverna above the harbour of Ormos, on the Aegean island of Agatos. At forty-nine, three years a widow, she wants nothing to change: not the eight tables under the vine, not her daughter Daphne beside her in the kitchen, not the quiet in the house after dark.

Then a Bulgarian cargo ship, the Kaliakra, steams into the bay on the last voyage of her captain. Stefan Marinov was a young officer in the summer of 1998, when a damaged rudder kept his ship in port for eight days and Eleni fell in love. Now he looks across the terrace at a twenty-seven-year-old with a jaw he knows, and he begins to count the years.

Eleni has a business to run, a stubborn father who never forgave the sea, and a daughter who is quietly dreaming of a life in Athens. Stefan has a retirement date, a son who says he comes home only as a guest, and a ship that cannot leave while the quarry crane stays broken. Every day the island holds him, Eleni must choose: keep the silence she has built her life around, or risk the very people it was meant to protect.

Twenty-eight years of saying nothing have left everyone waiting on a different quay. How long can one late summer hold its breath?
